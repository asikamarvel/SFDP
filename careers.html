Social Media Manager

SFDP is hiring a paid, contract Social Media Manager to lead our online presence across Instagram, Facebook, X, TikTok, LinkedIn, and YouTube. You'll plan and publish content, grow our community, and help translate our health education work into stories that reach more people.

  • Build and manage a monthly content calendar across all SFDP platforms
  • Create and edit graphics, short-form video, and captions aligned with our brand
  • Track engagement and growth metrics, and report on what's working
  • Coordinate with our volunteer content and outreach teams
